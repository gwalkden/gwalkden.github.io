  • Emerging Voices Corpus

The Emerging Voices Corpus is a small corpus (47,481 words; 53,567 tokens including punctuation) of Early Modern English (1500-1800), constructed as part of a course on Early Modern English taught at the University of Konstanz in summer 2019.
