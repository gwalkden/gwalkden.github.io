Technical details

Registration

You can register here. The registration deadline is May 1st 2021.

Software and hosting

The conference and workshop will take place using Zoom (for the talks) and Gather (for the breaks and the poster session). Registered participants will receive the links to the Zoom and Gather spaces before the conference begins.

Code of conduct

As a small community that has always been friendly and welcoming, we do not have a bespoke code of conduct for this meeting. We do, however, urge you to look at the codes of conduct adopted by other conferences:

  • DGfS 2021 Code of Conduct
  • LSA Code of Conduct

Participants are asked to reflect on whether their past behaviour could be construed as unacceptable in any of the ways listed here, and to try to ensure that this does not happen. The organizers reserve the right to turf out anyone whose behaviour they view as unacceptable at any point.
